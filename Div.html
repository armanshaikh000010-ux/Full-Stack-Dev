London is the capital and largest city of England and the United Kingdom, standing as a premier global hub of history, finance, and culture. Founded by the Romans nearly 2,000 years ago as Londinium, the city sits along the River Thames in southeast England.
         Today, it spans an area of approximately 607 square miles and is home to a diverse population of over 9 million people, with its wider metropolitan area reaching 15.4 million residents
